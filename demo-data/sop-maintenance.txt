# MANGALORE REFINERY AND PETROCHEMICALS LIMITED (MRPL)
## STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: MECHANICAL EQUIPMENT MAINTENANCE & CORROSION REPAIR
**Document Reference:** MRPL-SOP-MNT-2024-04
**Classification:** INTERNAL CONFIDENTIAL - REFINERY OPERATIONS

SECTION 1: PURPOSE & SCOPE
This procedure applies to pressure vessels, heat exchangers, and piping circuits operating in Crude Distillation Units (CDU), Fluidized Catalytic Cracking Units (FCCU), and Hydrocracker Units (HCU).

SECTION 2: MINIMUM ALLOWABLE WALL THICKNESS (T-MIN) STANDARDS
Per ASME Section VIII Div 1 & API 510 inspection standards, any pressure vessel component exhibiting wall thickness below calculated T-min must be classified immediately as a CRITICAL SAFETY HAZARD.

SECTION 3: EQUIPMENT CORROSION & DEFECT PROTOCOL
Equipment ID: EX-402A / EX-402B (Crude Pre-Heat Exchangers)
- Nominal Design Thickness: 10.00 mm
- Minimum Allowable Thickness (T-min): 4.50 mm
- Maximum Allowable Operating Pressure (MAOP): 18.5 bar at 280°C

SECTION 4.2: EMERGENCY REPAIR PROTOCOL FOR T-MIN DEFICIT
1. When measured wall thickness is <= 4.50 mm:
   a. Immediately flag equipment for CRITICAL ISOLATION.
   b. Initiate weld overlay patch or complete shell segment replacement.
   c. Perform 100% Radiographic Testing (RT) and Ultrasonic Testing (UT) per NDT Protocol NDT-UT-09.
   d. Prepare an official Engineering Approval Note for Chief Reliability Engineer signoff prior to restart.
